Inventory Features

Stock Level Monitoring

Real-time tracking of pharmaceutical stock levels across all distribution warehouses. Automatic alerts when inventory falls below minimum thresholds.

Lot Tracking

Comprehensive lot number tracking for full traceability of pharmaceutical products. Links inventory to purchase orders and shipments for complete audit trails.

Expiry Monitoring

Automated expiration date monitoring with alerts for items approaching or past expiration. First-Expire-First-Out (FIFO) support for optimal inventory rotation.

Cold Chain Management

Special handling for temperature-sensitive pharmaceutical products. Temperature control requirements and status tracking for cold chain items.

Multi-Warehouse Support

Manage inventory across multiple distribution centers. Transfer inventory between warehouses and track location-specific stock levels.

Supplier Management

Maintain supplier information and ratings. Track purchase orders and supplier performance metrics.

Inventory Overview

Access comprehensive inventory management tools through the menu above. Use the Product Search to find specific items and view their current stock levels.
